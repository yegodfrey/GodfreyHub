# 生成签名证书

1. [打开项目](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/quickApp-Guides/quickgame-tool-open-0000001616248428)进入工具主界面后，在顶部菜单栏选择"工具 \> 生成证书"。

   ![](https://alliance-communityfile-drcn.dbankcdn.com/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20260121135240.14087271015064015553122564451070:50001231000000:2800:E08D415AADE9E972747802DAA66DF7FA52EA35236DABA20EA953FD1138538844.png)
2. 在弹出的"创建新的签名"窗口中点击"创建"。 ![](https://alliance-communityfile-drcn.dbankcdn.com/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20260121135240.97880251905582357628133185933347:50001231000000:2800:88A1222650D8B14DB2011A21F7F161AD24B009970B4AD824EA66A3CE7A580D27.png)

   若签名证书创建成功，将在右下角弹出"创建证书成功"的提示。

   ![](https://alliance-communityfile-drcn.dbankcdn.com/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20260121135240.59342022412950446258252440117172:50001231000000:2800:B14E571742E650FD439FE436052B42F4F01FC7D67FE7AE74242A4C8CE9A6BBB4.png)
3. 您可在主界面的顶部菜单栏选择"工具 \> 生成证书"查看生成的签名信息。 ![](https://alliance-communityfile-drcn.dbankcdn.com/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20260121135240.58519092163045586388155613156097:50001231000000:2800:3259317AA09B381A85872A759159A6D190C31C8CD0C31EFAD008BA4E9A6EC4D3.png)

|Class Info|

|Location|[getConvertingLocation](#section43512495554)() 获取触发时的位置信息。|
|GeofenceData|[getDataFromIntent](#section1166453019493)(Intent intent) 从intent中解析出GeofenceData对象。|
|int|[getErrorCode](#section12130104713499)() 获取错误码。|
|boolean|[isFailure](#section132428605010)() 是否是异常触发围栏事件。|

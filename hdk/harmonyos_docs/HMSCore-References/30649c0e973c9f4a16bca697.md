# HWGroundOverlay

## 概述

在地图上指定区域显示图像。

## 构造函数

|函数|**描述**|
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|HWMapJsSDK.HWGroundOverlay(imageUrl, bounds, groundOverlayOptions)|* imageUrl：图片的URL地址。 * bounds：[GroundOverLayLatLngBounds](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-params-0000001051070092#section1740653517)显示的填充区域。 * groundOverlayOptions：设置[HWGroundOverlay](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwgroundoverlay-0000001050710116)的属性，详情见[GroundOverlayOptions](#section1490754316335)。|

## 方法

|**方法**|**描述**|**参数类型**|**返回值**|
|:------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|getBounds()|图片显示的范围。|-|[GroundOverLayLatLngBounds](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-params-0000001051070092#section1740653517)|
|getMap()|获取绑定的地图实例。|-|[HWMap](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwmap-0000001050990165)|
|getOpacity()|获取不透明度。|-|Number|
|getUrl()|获取图片地址。|-|String|
|getZIndex()|获取[HWGroundOverlay](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwgroundoverlay-0000001050710116)的z-index属性。|-|Number|
|setBounds(bounds)|设置图片的显示范围。|[GroundOverLayLatLngBounds](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-params-0000001051070092#section1740653517)|-|
|setMap(map)|绑定到地图。|[HWMap](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwmap-0000001050990165)|-|
|setOpacity(opacity)|修改不透明度。|Number|-|
|setUrl(url)|更新图片地址。|String|-|
|setZIndex(zIndex)|设置[HWGroundOverlay](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwgroundoverlay-0000001050710116)的z-index属性，即z轴方向的叠加关系。|Number|-|

## GroundOverlayOptions

|**参数**|**是否必选**|**参数类型**|**描述**|
|:------|:-------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|map|是|[HWMap](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwmap-0000001050990165)|地图实例。|
|opacity|否|Number|不透明度，取值范围：[0, 1]。 * 0：完全不透明。 * 1：完全透明。 默认值为0。|
|zIndex|否|Number|[HWGroundOverlay](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwgroundoverlay-0000001050710116)的z-index属性，即z轴方向的叠加关系（仅相对于其他[HWGroundOverlay](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/js-hwgroundoverlay-0000001050710116)）。|

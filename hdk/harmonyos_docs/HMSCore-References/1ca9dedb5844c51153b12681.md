# TimeCategories

* 支持的场景：手机。
* 支持的OS：EMUI 7.0及以上，Android 7.0及以上。

|Interface Info|
|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public interfaceTimeCategories 表示当前时间和位置的语义时间状态的接口类。时间状态是TimeBarrier类中定义的上午[TIME_CATEGORY_MORNING](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section10271398168)，下午[TIME_CATEGORY_AFTERNOON](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section191005571097)，晚上[TIME_CATEGORY_EVENING](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section1668363820154)，夜间[TIME_CATEGORY_NIGHT](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section108974225166)，工作日[TIME_CATEGORY_WEEKDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section291914399163)，休息日[TIME_CATEGORY_WEEKEND](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section12936145791617)，节假日[TIME_CATEGORY_HOLIDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section19935165181512)，非节假日[TIME_CATEGORY_NOT_HOLIDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section4591131316174)。|

## Public Methods

### getTimeCategories

|Method|
|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public abstract int[] getTimeCategories() 获取用户当前所在地的时间状态。返回的是一个数组，数组中的每个数值都对应TimeBarrier类中定义的一个时间状态常量：上午[TIME_CATEGORY_MORNING](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section10271398168)，下午[TIME_CATEGORY_AFTERNOON](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section191005571097)，晚上[TIME_CATEGORY_EVENING](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section1668363820154)，夜间[TIME_CATEGORY_NIGHT](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section108974225166)，工作日[TIME_CATEGORY_WEEKDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section291914399163)，休息日[TIME_CATEGORY_WEEKEND](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section12936145791617)，节假日[TIME_CATEGORY_HOLIDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section19935165181512)，非节假日[TIME_CATEGORY_NOT_HOLIDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section4591131316174)。例如返回数组为[1，5，7]，则表示的时间状态为[上午，节假日，休息日]。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:----|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|int[]|与适用于当前时间和地理位置的所有时间状态标签相对应的int常数数组。时间状态是TimeBarrier类中定义的 上午[TIME_CATEGORY_MORNING](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section10271398168)， 下午[TIME_CATEGORY_AFTERNOON](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section191005571097)， 晚上[TIME_CATEGORY_EVENING](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section1668363820154)， 夜间[TIME_CATEGORY_NIGHT](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section108974225166)， 工作日[TIME_CATEGORY_WEEKDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section291914399163)， 休息日[TIME_CATEGORY_WEEKEND](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section12936145791617)， 节假日[TIME_CATEGORY_HOLIDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section19935165181512)， 非节假日[TIME_CATEGORY_NOT_HOLIDAY](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-timebarrier-0000001050166041#section4591131316174)。|

### isTimeCategory

|Method|
|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public abstract boolean isTimeCategory(int desiredTimeCategory) 判断当前的时间状态信息是否包含传入的时间状态标签。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:------------------|:----------|
|desiredTimeCategory|时间状态标签。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:------|:--------------------------------------|
|boolean|如果当前的时间状态信息包含传入的时间状态，则返回true；否则返回false。|

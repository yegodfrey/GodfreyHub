* 支持的OS：EMUI 7.0及以上，Android 7.0及以上。

## Public Methods

### keep

|Method|
|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public static [AwarenessBarrier](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-awarenessbarrier-0000001050164120) keep(int deviceType, int bluetoothStatus) 蓝牙状态驻留围栏，开发者添加连接或非连接两种状态的蓝牙状态驻留围栏后，当蓝牙状态保持为指定状态时，此围栏状态为TRUE并触发围栏事件上报。 使用此围栏需添加android.permission.BLUETOOTH权限。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:--------------|:-----------------------------------------------|
|deviceType|蓝牙设备类型。 DEVICE_CAR：标识车载蓝牙设备。|
|bluetoothStatus|车载蓝牙状态。 CONNECTED：车载蓝牙已连接。 DISCONNECTED：车载蓝牙未连接。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------|
|[AwarenessBarrier](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-References/awareness-barrier-awarenessbarrier-0000001050164120)|情景感知围栏。|

**Throws**

|Name|Description|
|:-----------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|IllegalArgumentException|参数不合法（deviceType不是非负整数，bluetoothStatus不是CONNECTED或DISCONNECTED或UNKNOWN）异常。|

### connecting

|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Properties

|名称|类型|描述|
|:------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------|
|bearing|double|相机的朝向。|
|target|[LatLng](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/flutter-plugin-latlng-0000001252864733)|相机指向位置的经纬度。|

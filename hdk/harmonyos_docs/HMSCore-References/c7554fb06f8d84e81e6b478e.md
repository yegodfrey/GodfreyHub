|200|OK|0|请求成功。|-|

|404|NOT_FOUND|5|请求的URL有问题。|请确认请求的URL与发布的是否一致。|
|401|REQUEST_DENIED|6|token非法或者失效。|[请参见FAQ。](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/faq-0000001187275650#section224819328403)|

|401|REQUEST_DENIED|10003|缺少接口调用权限。|[请参见FAQ](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/faq-0000001187275650#section1817194262911)。|

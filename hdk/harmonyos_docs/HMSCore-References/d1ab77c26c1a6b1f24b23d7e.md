### getChannelInfo

|Method|
|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public String getChannelInfo() throws android.os.RemoteException 获取转化跟踪参数。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:-----|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|String|返回转化跟踪参数。 JSON格式：{"channelInfo":"http%3a%2f%2fwww.huawei.com%2fappgalley%2fc001231", "clickTimestamp":1561820232123,"installTimestamp":1561820266321"}， 其中channelInfo是转化跟踪参数，clickTimestamp是点击广告的时间戳，installTimestamp是安装App的时间戳。|

**Throws**

|Name|Description|
|:--------------|:-----------|
|RemoteException|远程服务发生错误时抛出。|

# PKIXReason

[java.lang.Object](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/object-0000001054678446) |---[java.lang.Enum](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/enum-0000001054838398)<[PKIXReason](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/pkixreason-0000001054838496)> |---|---java.security.cert.PKIXReason
    
    public enum PKIXReason
    extends [Enum](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/enum-0000001054838398)<[PKIXReason](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/pkixreason-0000001054838496)>
    implements [CertPathValidatorException.Reason](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/certpathvalidatorexception_reason-0000001054358515)  
  
---  
The PKIXReason enumerates the potential PKIX-specific reasons that an X.509 certification path may be invalid according to the PKIX (RFC 5280) standard. These reasons are in addition to those of the CertPathValidatorException.BasicReason enumeration.

**Since:**

1.7

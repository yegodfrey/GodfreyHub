Enumeration of the style of a localized date, time or date-time formatter. 

These styles are used when obtaining a date-time style from configuration. See [DateTimeFormatter](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/datetimeformatter-0000001054678545) and [DateTimeFormatterBuilder](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/datetimeformatterbuilder-0000001054838530) for usage.

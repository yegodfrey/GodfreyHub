Localized definitions of the day-of-week, week-of-month and week-of-year fields.  A standard week is seven days long, but cultures have different definitions for some other aspects of a week. This class represents the definition of the week, for the purpose of providing [TemporalField](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/temporalfield-0000001054439865) instances.  WeekFields provides five fields, [dayOfWeek()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__dayOfWeek--), [weekOfMonth()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__weekOfMonth--), [weekOfYear()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__weekOfYear--), [weekOfWeekBasedYear()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__weekOfWeekBasedYear--), and [weekBasedYear()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__weekBasedYear--) that provide access to the values from any [Temporal](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/temporal-0000001054838532).  The computations for day-of-week, week-of-month, and week-of-year are based on the [ChronoField#YEAR](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/chronofield-0000001054119838#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054119838__YEAR), [ChronoField#MONTH_OF_YEAR](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/chronofield-0000001054119838#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054119838__MONTH_OF_YEAR), [ChronoField#DAY_OF_MONTH](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/chronofield-0000001054119838#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054119838__DAY_OF_MONTH), and [ChronoField#DAY_OF_WEEK](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/chronofield-0000001054119838#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054119838__DAY_OF_WEEK) which are based on the [ChronoField#EPOCH_DAY](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/chronofield-0000001054119838#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054119838__EPOCH_DAY) and the chronology. The values may not be aligned with the [ChronoField#YEAR_OF_ERA](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/chronofield-0000001054119838#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054119838__YEAR_OF_ERA) depending on the Chronology.  A week is defined by: 
    * The first day-of-week. For example, the ISO-8601 standard considers Monday to be the first day-of-week. 
    * The minimal number of days in the first week. For example, the ISO-8601 standard counts the first week as needing at least 4 days. 
Together these two values allow a year or month to be divided into weeks. **Week of Month** One field is used: week-of-month. The calculation ensures that weeks never overlap a month boundary. The month is divided into periods where each period starts on the defined first day-of-week. The earliest period is referred to as week 0 if it has less than the minimal number of days and week 1 if it has at least the minimal number of days. **Week of Year** One field is used: week-of-year. The calculation ensures that weeks never overlap a year boundary. The year is divided into periods where each period starts on the defined first day-of-week. The earliest period is referred to as week 0 if it has less than the minimal number of days and week 1 if it has at least the minimal number of days. **Week Based Year** Two fields are used for week-based-year, one for the [week-of-week-based-year](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__weekOfWeekBasedYear--) and one for [week-based-year](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/weekfields-0000001054358568#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054358568__weekBasedYear--). In a week-based-year, each week belongs to only a single year. Week 1 of a year is the first week that starts on the first day-of-week and has at least the minimum number of days. The first and last weeks of a year may contain days from the previous calendar year or next calendar year respectively. **Since:** 1.8Field Summary Modifier and Type | Field | Description

Enumerates the [RunningLock](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/powermanager_runninglock-0000001054678937) types. 

Two [RunningLock](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/powermanager_runninglock-0000001054678937) types are available: BACKGROUND, and PROXIMITY_SCREEN_CONTROL. BACKGROUND ensures that applications can run in the background. PROXIMITY_SCREEN_CONTROL determines whether to turn on or off the screen based on the proximity sensor.

#### [h2]PROXIMITY_SCREEN_CONTROL

public static final [PowerManager.RunningLockType](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/powermanager_runninglocktype-0000001054120297) PROXIMITY_SCREEN_CONTROL

Indicates the lock that determines whether to turn on or off the screen based on the proximity sensor. For example, during a call, if the proximity sensor detects that the device is moving close to the user's ear, the screen turns off; if the proximity sensor detects that the device is moving away from the user's ear, the screen turns on.

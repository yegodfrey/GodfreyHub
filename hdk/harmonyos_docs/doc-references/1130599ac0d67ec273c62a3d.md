Method Detail [h2]getGuarantor [Principal](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/principal-0000001054678520) getGuarantor​() Deprecated.  Returns the guarantor of the certificate, that is, the principal guaranteeing that the public key associated with this certificate is that of the principal associated with this certificate. For X.509 certificates, the guarantor will typically be a Certificate Authority (such as the United States Postal Service or Verisign, Inc.). **Returns:** the guarantor which guaranteed the principal-key binding. Returns the principal of the principal-key pair being guaranteed by the guarantor. **Returns:** the principal to which this certificate is bound. Returns the key of the principal-key pair being guaranteed by the guarantor. **Returns:** the public key that this certificate certifies belongs to a particular principal.

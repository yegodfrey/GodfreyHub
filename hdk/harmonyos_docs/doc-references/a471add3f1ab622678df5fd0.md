Stores the division of internal text directions of a section. [BidiStrategy](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/bidianalyzer_bidistrategy-0000001217877965) determines the general direction of the text in a section, but the direction of each language prevails within the section. For example, when English and Arabic texts coexist, the text direction of English is LTR, and the text direction of Arabic is RTL. Either of them is called a [BidiRun](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/bidianalyzer_bidirun-0000001172716654). **Since:** 7Constructor Summary Constructor | Description

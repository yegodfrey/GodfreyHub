Inherits from [SensorData](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/sensordata-0000001054838938) and describes data information of sensors in the other category (a category other than motion, environment, orientation, and light). The data information includes the timestamps, accuracy, data values, and data dimensions of sensors.

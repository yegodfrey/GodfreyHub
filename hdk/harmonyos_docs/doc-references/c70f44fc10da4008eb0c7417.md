A nexus for I/O operations.  A channel represents an open connection to an entity such as a hardware device, a file, a network socket, or a program component that is capable of performing one or more distinct I/O operations, for example reading or writing.  A channel is either open or closed. A channel is open upon creation, and once closed it remains closed. Once a channel is closed, any attempt to invoke an I/O operation upon it will cause a [ClosedChannelException](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/closedchannelexception-0000001054838448) to be thrown. Whether or not a channel is open may be tested by invoking its [isOpen](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/channel-0000001054838442#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054838442__isOpen--) method.

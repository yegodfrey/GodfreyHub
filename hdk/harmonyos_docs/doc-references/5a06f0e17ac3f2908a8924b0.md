# CvRect

[java.lang.Object](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/object-0000001054678446) |---ohos.ai.cv.common.CvRect
    
    public class CvRect
    extends [Object](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/object-0000001054678446)
    implements [Sequenceable](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/sequenceable-0000001054239001)  
  
---  
Describes the rectangle area of images. 

The rectangle area has four coordinates (in pixels): vertical coordinate of the upper boundary, horizontal coordinate of the left boundary, vertical coordinate of the lower boundary, and horizontal coordinate of the right boundary.

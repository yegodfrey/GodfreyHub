Describes video properties. 
    * The [getRecorderFps()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderFps--) method obtains the video frame capture rate. 
    * The [getRecorderDegrees()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderDegrees--) method obtains the video rotation angle. 
    * The [getRecorderRate()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderRate--) method obtains the video frame rate. 
    * The [getRecorderWidth()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderWidth--) method obtains the video width. 
    * The [getRecorderHeight()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderHeight--) method obtains the video height. 
    * The [getRecorderVideoEncoder()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderVideoEncoder--) method obtains the video encoding format. 
    * The [getRecorderBitRate()](https://developer.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/doc-references/videoproperty-0000001054518938#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001054518938__getRecorderBitRate--) method obtains the video encoding bit rate.

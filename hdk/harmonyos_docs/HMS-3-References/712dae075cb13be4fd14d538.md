华为游戏服务包

## 接口

接口名| 描述  
---|---  
[Games](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/games-v3 "Games")| 获取游戏实例的相关接口。  
[GamesClient](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/gamesclient-v3 "GamesClient")| 游戏基础能力相关接口。  
[PlayersClient](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/playersclient-v3 "PlayersClient")| 玩家信息相关接口。  
[AchievementsClient](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/achievementsclient-v3 "AchievementsClient")| 成就管理相关接口。  
[EventsClient](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/eventsclient-v3 "EventsClient")| 事件管理相关接口。  
  
## 数据类

类名| 描述  
---|---  
[Player](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/player-v3 "Player")| Player类主要用于返回游戏玩家的具体信息。  
[PlayerLevelInfo](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/playerlevelInfo-v3 "PlayerLevelInfo")| PlayerLevelInfo类用于返回玩家等级信息。  
[PlayerLevel](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/playerlevel-v3 "PlayerLevel")| PlayerLevel类返回玩家当前等级的具体数字信息。  
[AnnotatedData](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/annotateddata-v3 "AnnotatedData")| AnnotatedData类用于缓存成就信息。  
[AchievementBuffer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/achievementbuffer-v3 "AchievementBuffer")| AchievementBuffer类返回玩家的成就列表信息。  
[Achievement](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/achievement-v3 "Achievement")| Achievement类返回具体成就信息  
[EventBuffer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/eventbuffer-v3)| EventBuffer类返回玩家的事件列表信息。  
  
[Event](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-3-References/event-v3)| Event类返回具体事件信息。

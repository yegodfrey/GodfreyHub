### getRequestStatus

|Method|
|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public [Result.STATUS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397) getRequestStatus(long requestId) 根据requestId获取请求任务的状态，返回值包括：[INIT](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section1219017112356)、[INVALID](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section103169535297)、[PAUSE](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section147961123123015)、[PROCESS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section1131023314428)。请求任务完成后会先回调[Callback](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-callback-0000001077821946)的[onSuccess](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-callback-0000001077821946#section155564414510)方法再改变状态，所以在[onSuccess](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-callback-0000001077821946#section155564414510)中获取的状态仍为[PROCESS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section1131023314428)。 先回调再改变状态的原因是，若先改变状态再回调，在回调之前进程存在被终止的可能，此时您并未收到成功的回调，但状态已变为已完成，导致状态错乱。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:--------|:----------|
|requestId|请求ID。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|[Result.STATUS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397)|请求任务的状态。 * [INIT](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section1219017112356)：请求任务在请求队列中，待执行 * [INVALID](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section103169535297)：请求任务不存在或已经执行完成 * [PAUSE](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section147961123123015)：请求任务已暂停 * [PROCESS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-References/network-file-api-enum-status-0000001156554397#section1131023314428)：请求任务正在执行中|

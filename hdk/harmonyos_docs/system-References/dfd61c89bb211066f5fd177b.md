### acceptConnect

|Method|
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public abstract [Task](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hmscore-common-References/task_tresult-0000001050121148)<Void> acceptConnect(String endpointId, [DataCallback](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/datacallback-0000001050130654) dataCallback) 接受远端的连接。连接的两端都调用此接口，才能建立连接。调用[requestConnectEx](#section5430634112314)(String, String, [ConnectCallback](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/connectcallback-0000001050132589)，[ConnectOption](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/android-discovery-connectoption-0000001075801928))发起建连8秒内，如果未调用此接口接受连接则连接失败，需要重新发起建连。API执行返回的主要状态码包括： * [STATUS_SUCCESS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section4767319183113)：接受此连接。 * [STATUS_ALREADY_CONNECTED](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section36742152126)：应用已经连接到该端点。 * [STATUS_WIFI_MUST_BE_ENABLED](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section17198428456)：Wi-Fi未打开。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:-----------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|endpointId|远端设备的endpointId。此值应与[onEstablish](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/connectcallback-0000001050132589#section1487514421181)(String, String, [ConnectCallback](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/connectcallback-0000001050132589))中的值保持一致。|
|dataCallback|与远端进行数据传输回调，用于数据接收或者收发进度通知。|

|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

|option|广播选项。通过[BroadcastOption.Builder](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/broadcastoption-builder-0000001050132581)构造类构造option，并设置[Policy](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/discovery-policy-0000001050132587)。广播选项的策略应与扫描选项的策略保持一致，否则无法被发现。|

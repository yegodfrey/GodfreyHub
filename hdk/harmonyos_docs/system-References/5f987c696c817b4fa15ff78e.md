### sendData(List<String> endpointIds, Data data)

|Method|
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public abstract [Task](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hmscore-common-References/task_tresult-0000001050121148)<Void> sendData(List<String> endpointIds, [Data](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/data-0000001050132599) data) 是上述方法的变种，提供了一个endpoint的列表，给多个设备同时发送数据。 只有连接建立成功才可以调用此接口发送数据，返回状态描述如下： * [STATUS_API_DISORDER](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section117291737193416)：调用乱序。 * [STATUS_ENDPOINT_UNKNOWN](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section494551712196)：有效连接未建立。 * [STATUS_SUCCESS](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section4767319183113)：成功，此处成功只表示开始发送数据，并不代表数据发送完成，数据发送状态通过后续的onDataTransferUpdate通知。 * [STATUS_MISSING_PERMISSION_FILE_READ_WRITE](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/stauscode-0000001050132575#section611311191718)：文件读写权限缺失。 > 说明 > 调用此接口时，[STREAM](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/data-type-0000001050132603#section1613311470194)类型数据并发发送数量不能大于8个，[BYTES](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/data-type-0000001050132603#section8206112311193)和[FILE](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/system-References/data-type-0000001050132603#section181981039201911)类型数据并发发送数量不能大于100个。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|

|data|将要发送的数据。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------------------------|

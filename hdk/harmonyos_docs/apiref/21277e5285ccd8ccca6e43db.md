#### Overview

**Related Modules:**

Defines the PWM device configuration parameters. 

Attention

#### Details

#### Field

#### [h2]duty
    
    uint32_t PwmConfig::duty

**Description:**

Duty cycle, in nanoseconds 

#### [h2]number
    
    uint32_t PwmConfig::number

**Description:**

Number of square waves to generate. A positive value indicates that the specified number of square waves will be generated, and **0** indicates that square waves will be continuously generated. 

#### [h2]period
    
    uint32_t PwmConfig::period

**Description:**

PWM period, in nanoseconds 

#### [h2]polarity
    
    uint8_t PwmConfig::polarity

**Description:**

Polarity ------------------— | ------------------— PWM_NORMAL_POLARITY | Normal polarity PWM_INVERTED_POLARITY | Inverted polarity 

#### [h2]status
    
    uint8_t PwmConfig::status

**Description:**

Running status ---------------— | ---------------— PWM_DISABLE_STATUS | Disabled PWM_ENABLE_STATUS | Enabled

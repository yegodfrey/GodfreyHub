# 通过Remote-SSH远程连接失败，日志显示“Bad owner or permissions on C:\\\Users\\\xxx/.ssh/config”

#### 问题现象

通过VSCode中的Remote Explorer或者通过CMD等命令行窗口连接远程服务器时，连接失败，提示"Could not establish connection"，日志信息显示 "Bad owner or permissions on C:\\\Users\\\xxx/.ssh/config"。

#### 解决措施

     1. 在VSCode左侧边栏点击![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20240930102432.04999078788770026648562308182781:50001231000000:2800:5D728ACB9648327544784C0388055412933A85A613A39B34A8DE9FADBB6FF1DC.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)，在展开的已安装的插件中找到Remote-SSH，点击![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20240930102432.96733455554092923996637300282803:50001231000000:2800:3F6DD8C574AA3539400A5F994841E0DBC26C4AABC3F87185C6208237988B1370.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)，在展开的菜单中，点击Extension Settings。如下图所示。

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20240930102432.31965341216738312068385141346988:50001231000000:2800:9C0E7F68AF20CCD5518969CF772AE9C11954844B43A654CF5F0F44CB4049330E.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

     2. 在弹出的Settings页面，填写SSH配置文件的地址，SSH配置文件通常为C:\Users\username\\.ssh\config。

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20240930102432.57050823389829663977367299999297:50001231000000:2800:BC11034C78B9BD20BCE4D6FA827134584B404B44E39A323187A633E80381AC8F.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

     3. 参考远程[连接Ubuntu环境](https://device.harmonyos.com/cn/docs/documentation/guide/ide-install-windows-ubuntu-0000001194073744#section128105597379)重新连接远程服务器端。

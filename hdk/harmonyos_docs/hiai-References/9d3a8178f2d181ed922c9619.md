### setPerformanceType(int performanceType)

|Method|
|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public [ML3DFaceAnalyzerSetting.Factory](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hiai-References/ml3dfaceanalyzersetting-factory-0000001061448479) setPerformanceType(int performanceType) 设置检测器偏好模式。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:--------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|performanceType|检测器偏好模式。 * [TYPE_PRECISION](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hiai-References/ml3dfaceanalyzersetting-0000001062408450#section289934410414)：精度偏好模式，保持送测图片分辨率不变，检测到的人脸更多，位置点信息更加准确，但是检测速度更慢（默认值）。 * [TYPE_SPEED](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hiai-References/ml3dfaceanalyzersetting-0000001062408450#section13031340192219)：速度偏好模式，对送测图片进行降采样，在确定诸如关键点位置之类的值时可能精度较低，但是检测速度更快。|

### setTracingAllowed(boolean tracingAllowed)

|Method|
|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public [ML3DFaceAnalyzerSetting.Factory](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hiai-References/ml3dfaceanalyzersetting-factory-0000001061448479) setTracingAllowed(boolean tracingAllowed) 设置是否启用人脸跟踪功能。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:-------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|tracingAllowed|是否启用人脸跟踪功能。 * true：启用人脸跟踪功能，视频流过程中打开，检测到人脸后开始跟踪，运行速度更快（默认值）。 * false：不启用人脸跟踪功能，对每次送检图片都进行人脸检测，静态图片检测时使用。|

**Returns**

|Type|Description|
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------------------------------|

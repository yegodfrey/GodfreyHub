# DynamicShapeConfig

![](https://alliance-communityfile-drcn.dbankcdn.com/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20260104150638.18869693346848568443091493105274:50001231000000:2800:4EA4BCA46AF94905299783319536A39339893CDB3F0CDCC3E275A27AD8C12B79.png)  
此接口从DDK 5.0.1.0版本起废弃，请使用模型管家V1接口[DynamicShapeConfig](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/hiai-References/dynamicshapeconfig-0000001281339986)替代。  

|Struct Info|
|:----------------------------------------|
|struct DynamicShapeConfig 编译前可变shape配置选项。|

|Qualifier and Type|Field and Description|
|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------|
|bool|enable 是否使能编译前可变shape，默认值为false，不使能。 * true：使能。 * false：不使能。|
|uint32_t|maxCachedNum 最大缓存数目，取值区间\[2,10\]。|
|[CacheMode](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/hiai-References/cachemode-0000001092518368)|cacheMode 缓存模式，默认值为CACHE_BUILDED_MODEL。|

### setLanguageList(List<String> languageList)

|Method|
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|public [MLRemoteTextSetting.Factory](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/hiai-References/mlremtexsetfac-harmonyos-0000001200957824) setLanguageList(List<String> languageList) 设置预置语种，正确地预置分析语种，可以使检测更快速更准确；在不确定语种的情况下，可以不设置，服务会自动识别语种。|

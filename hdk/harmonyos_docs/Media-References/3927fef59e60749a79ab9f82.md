### KEY_VIDEO_RATIO

### KEY_BITMAP_RECYCLE

|Field|

|String KEY_BITMAP_RECYCLE 位图回收开关，通过[setValue](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/Media-References/api-localinterface-0000001050126365#section142051024567)设置为[VALUE_TRUE](#section4851010185610)时，表示通过[setImage](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/Media-References/api-localinterface-0000001050126365#section79943444187)设置的位图在被加载成纹理后，会被回收，设置为[VALUE_FALSE](#section91158527566)时，该位图不会被回收，需要调用者根据实际情况进行释放回收。默认状况请参见[setImage](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/Media-References/api-localinterface-0000001050126365#section79943444187)。注：该属性须在[setImage](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/Media-References/api-localinterface-0000001050126365#section79943444187)前调用才能生效。 另请参阅：[Constant-values](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/Media-References/api-constant-values-0000001053806132)。|

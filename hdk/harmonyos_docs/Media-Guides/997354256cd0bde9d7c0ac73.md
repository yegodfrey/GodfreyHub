# BlueCat插件说明

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.20314605109391699615432772246045:50001231000000:2800:3E28692146D9C3B53D38DC7FB1C04A4EDA013A86173289FCAE7AFD2000A9CCB1.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)  
仅限用于虚拟声卡模式。  

#### 适配场景

在虚拟声卡模式下，音频通过Pro Tools总线输出给虚拟声卡传输到Renderer上，位置信息则通过Panner传输给Renderer。BlueCat插件只支持单声道和立体声，Panner只能使用单声道和立体声模式。  

#### 用途

通过BlueCat桥接方式将Panner插件挂载在Pro Tools轨道上，让工具可以在Pro Tools中加载使用。  

#### 挂载方法

1. Pro Tools创建音频轨道，点击菜单栏中的"轨道"（快捷键是"Command+Shift+N"），弹出"新建轨道"的窗口。
2. 选择新建轨道的类型和形式并填写轨道名称。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.26420951962948003608778930966063:50001231000000:2800:A9CEC30B8B79A32306409F2A2EF1FA6EDFCAE87C7748D3D1E136A2F3896817FF.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
3. 在Pro Tools单声道或立体声轨道上挂载BlueCat插件。
4. 点击"插件 \> 其他 \> BlueCat Patchwork（单声道）"。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.48318274897182817294932949471786:50001231000000:2800:77C204906519C7B00D2F91065C309A648F14E3AB0829C98227AD601D5A772635.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
5. 在BlueCat上选择"Default"模式，选择"Load VST3"，找到"PetalVividPanner"所在路径并打开。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.62563204022184060121528895657912:50001231000000:2800:86B154DDF74302CBB6268C9038106AE28A23ACB2F0251EA1D3C62204422B8B7A.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.97969923593944271941319222207774:50001231000000:2800:A6AB0DCB1F6AACBD4BF8492DBE8FCE3E1B4D58DBEA6CD51DF238A87BA7921F07.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
6. 挂载完Panner后，Panner界面即可呈现。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.80721232361269040370165888919137:50001231000000:2800:D9CC89AB4669EE242E78103ABC633145B2A1C03B70CBE052FE493861FE018D4B.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
7. 播放引擎设置，点击"设置"，选择"播放引擎"，选择设备"Petal Audio Bridge''。硬件缓冲大小推荐1024。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.20382180475378481570043370106807:50001231000000:2800:2447C5836AB33AC41D47F24DA4D3DFACE79BF591B29A1832113A798E5044B715.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.71958897322549812612735646812000:50001231000000:2800:B55A53C91F23243F385E899BD627667AE8DE3F06011C05A86FD057EA1DF3C443.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
8. 选择"设置"，找到I/O，弹出I/O窗口。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.15040977669343372783620212651498:50001231000000:2800:89C676487AB8430BEB4ADE66CA0751A57B2D9D658C022E9B383E5EF26159565E.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

   点击"输出"并创建新路径，设置1个7.1.2输出、118个单声道输出、1个单声道输出作为LTC通道。设置完成后，总线界面将自动生成对应的总线设置。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.00066550737685335468984246324407:50001231000000:2800:56D01371607F0CFA1CB2EEBD6A9E13FF30D8FB2CF43D13A870D954270EDDF023.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.73072398525875228290078849337409:50001231000000:2800:3A894623A01C2F0F451EBF3248CB0793726258A72CC0D9E791C2F058040EC142.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")

   对输出进行设置，以7.1.2为例，创建7.1.2格式的路径作为静态声床，对应Renderer(1-10)静态声床通道，根据需要创建对象通道。创建路径后"总线"自动设置成功。之后在对应轨道选择相对应的总线输出。

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.42949794047782951011787418558938:50001231000000:2800:0FD09EE11BF6CF9F6571D431CFBB23E5E26B44A8EFAEA4D4972EE9D06B125A31.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")  

#### Automation数据读取/写入

1. 在BlueCat上挂载Panner后，点击Panner插件出现菜单，然后勾选"X"、"Y"、"Z"。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.01361470606552248978167641838414:50001231000000:2800:69643CCB89AAD7D9E7E70B7CA3BDE993A6FE6B7A4357056FC67D40983F623D01.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
2. 点击Pro Tools插件框上的"Auto \> Plug-In Automation enable \> Automation \> "设置界面，然后点击"Add"把"X"、"Y"、"Z"添加进去，最后点击"OK"完成。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152643.37566415691033405556840793219102:50001231000000:2800:DE6A6897DA7D0867B463333AE7B8F439BDA81E61680AAC98ACF4B515371027EA.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")
3. 点击轨道头上的Track View selector下拉箭头，添加自动化行，选择对应的"X"、"Y"、"Z"，然后进行编辑。

   ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230918152644.85546094936214083979240510495222:50001231000000:2800:7298CD97786AAE98D717E07675BD0595ACB9318F01FBB37AC087CEB3A9B72BFC.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true "点击放大")

   点击图片中的图标"显示/隐藏自动化行"，弹出下拉菜单，选择"Audio 1 （fx a）BlueCat's PatchWork"。

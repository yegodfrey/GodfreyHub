|Interface Info|
|:---------------------------------------------|
|public interface IInitCallback 设备虚拟化能力初始化回调接口。|

#### Public Method Summary

|Class|Description|
|:------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|abstract void|[onInitSuccess (VirtualizationAdapter adapter)](#section5689319185318) 接口用于服务对象初始化完成后触发回调，在回调的参数中返回实例化对象。|
|abstract void|[onInitFail (int error)](#section158023015415) 服务初始化失败时调用，携带错误码。|

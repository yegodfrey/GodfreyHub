函数 func sleep(Duration)
    
    public func sleep(dur: Duration): Unit

功能：休眠当前线程。 若 dur 小于等于 [Duration.Zero](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/openharmony-cangjie/time_package_structs#static-prop-zero)，当前线程会让出运行权。 参数：
    * dur: [Duration](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/openharmony-cangjie/time_package_structs#struct-duration) \- 线程休眠的时长。

# 错误码

|状态码|状态码描述|code|msg|描述|
|:--|:---------------------------------------------|:-----|:-------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|200|ok|0|ok|请求成功|
|200|ok|102010|无权限访问当前接口，请先开启接口访问权限|请先到AGC[开发与服务](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/service/josp/agc/index.html#/myProject)的API管理页，参见[API开通](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-ainetworking-serviceopen-0000002370503878#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370503878__li189184772911)，开启接口服务后再访问|
|400|Bad Request|10100|Bad Request|请求参数错误或格式不正确|
|401|verify the jwt failed/ can not get the sa info|无|无|jwt鉴权失败|
|404|404 Not Found|无|无|请求的资源不存在|
|500|Service inner error|10300|Service inner error|服务器内部错误|

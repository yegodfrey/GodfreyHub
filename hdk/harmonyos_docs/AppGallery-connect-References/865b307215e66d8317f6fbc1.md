## Methods

### credentialWithPassword

|Method|
|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|static credentialWithPassword(email:string, password:string):[AGConnectAuthCredential](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-References/harmonyos-ts-agconnectauthcredential-0000001471238990) 通过邮箱账号和密码获取凭证。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:-------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|email|邮箱账号。|
|password|密码。 默认密码规则如下，若您参考[安全配置](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-security-configuration-0000001324925609)自定义了密码复杂度请满足自定义的复杂度： 1. 密码长度至少8个字符。 2. 密码必须包含如下至少两种字符的组合： * 至少一个小写字母 * 至少一个大写字母 * 至少一个数字 * 至少一个特殊字符：`!@#$%^&*()-_=+\|[{}];:'",<.>/? 和空格 3. 密码不能和手机号码或者邮箱地址一样。|

**Return**

|Type|Description|
|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------|
|[AGConnectAuthCredential](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-References/harmonyos-ts-agconnectauthcredential-0000001471238990)|AGC凭证。|

**Sample Code**

```screen
EmailAuthProvider.credentialWithPassword('email', 'password')
```

### credentialWithPasswordAndVerifyCode

|Method|
|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|static credentialWithPasswordAndVerifyCode(email: string, password: string, verifyCode: string):[AGConnectAuthCredential](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-References/harmonyos-ts-agconnectauthcredential-0000001471238990) 通过邮箱账号和验证码获取凭证。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:---------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|email|邮箱账号。|
|password|密码（可选）。如果携带了密码，则密码和验证码都会校验。 默认密码规则如下，若您参考[安全配置](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-security-configuration-0000001324925609)自定义了密码复杂度请满足自定义的复杂度： 1. 密码长度至少8个字符。 2. 密码必须包含如下至少两种字符的组合： * 至少一个小写字母 * 至少一个大写字母 * 至少一个数字 * 至少一个特殊字符：`!@#$%^&*()-_=+\|[{}];:'",<.>/? 和空格 3. 密码不能和手机号码或者邮箱一样。|
|verifyCode|验证码。|

**Return**

|Type|Description|
|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------|
|[AGConnectAuthCredential](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-References/harmonyos-ts-agconnectauthcredential-0000001471238990)|AGC凭证。|

**Sample Code**

```screen
EmailAuthProvider.credentialWithPasswordAndVerifyCode('email', 'password', 'verifyCode')
```

|averageScore|O|Double|综合评分。|
|totalRatings|O|Long|总评分数量。|

|twoStarTimes|O|Integer|二星评分数量。|
|threeStarTimes|O|Integer|三星评分数量。|
|fourStarTimes|O|Integer|四星评分数量。|
|fiveStarTimes|O|Integer|五星评分数量。|

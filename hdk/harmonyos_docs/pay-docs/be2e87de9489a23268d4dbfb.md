签约、解约回调通知验签规则说明 签名前字符串拼接、使用字母序进行排序后进行验签。举例：
    
    排序前: {"templateNo":"100","openId":"***xx","signStatus":1,"resultCode":"000000","mercOrderNo":"***xx","signMode":"01","signNo":"***xx","resultDesc":"success","mercNo":"***xx","notifyType":0,"expireDate":"2099-12-31"}
    排序后:
    expireDate=2099-12-31&mercNo=***xx&mercOrderNo=***xx&notifyType=0&openId=***xx=&resultCode=000000&resultDesc=success&signMode=01&signNo=***xx&signStatus=1&templateNo=100

# GUI Layout

#### Projection Control

**HiSightSurfaceView** is a projection view provided by the HiCar SDK. After a connection is set up between a phone and the head unit, HiCar on the phone is projected to this view. After the HMI app integrates this view, HiCar apps and services can be displayed on the screen of the head unit. In addition, the touch operations the user performs on the view of the head unit screen are also transmitted to the phone side through the reverse control channel, so as to complete the user's control of the HiCar apps and services.

The **HiSightSurfaceView** is similar to other Android views. When integrating it to the projection screen of the HMI app, add the view description to the layout file. The following is the sample code:
    
    <RelativeLayout
            android:id="@+id/project_layout"
            android:layout_width="match_parent"
            android:layout_height="match_parent"
            android:gravity="center"
            android:background="#000000">
            **< com.huawei.hicarsdk.HiSightSurfaceView**
    **android:id="@+id/surface"**
    **android:focusableInTouchMode="true"**
    **android:layout_width="match_parent"**
    **android:layout_height="match_parent"**
    **android:fillViewport="true"**
    **android:scrollbars="vertical"/ >**
    </RelativeLayout>

#### UI Logic

When designing and developing HMI apps, follow the HUAWEI HiCar App UX Design Specification. The following describes the core interaction process and typical UIs.

#### [h2]Wireless Connection Process

Initial connection: After the head unit initiates a wireless connection, the user confirms the connection on the phone and enters the connection code. The HUAWEI HiCar screen is then displayed on the head unit.

Non-initial connection: When the phone approaches the car, the head unit will automatically enter the HUAWEI HiCar screen (when automatic connection is enabled and the user did not manually disconnect HUAWEI HiCar at last connection).

#### [h2]Wired Connection Process

Initial connection: Connect the phone to the car using a USB cable. After the connection is confirmed and authorized on the phone, the HUAWEI HiCar screen is displayed on the head unit.

Non-initial connection: After the phone is connected to the car via USB, the head unit will automatically enter the HUAWEI HiCar screen (when automatic connection is enabled).

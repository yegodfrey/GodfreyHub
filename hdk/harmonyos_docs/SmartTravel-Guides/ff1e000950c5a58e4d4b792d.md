# About the Service

#### Overview

HUAWEI HiCar (HiCar for short) is a man-car-home interconnection solution provided by Huawei for all scenarios. The solution has the following characteristics: 

     * Safety: Simplified interaction with safety as the prerequisite
     * Smart connection: Imperceptible interconnection between mobile phones/IoT devices and vehicles

     * Resource sharing: Resource sharing between mobile phones and vehicles

     * Seamless experience: Seamless ecosystem transfer between inside and outside vehicles

From the perspective of vehicles, the strength list includes multi-screen display (large screen for central control, dashboard, and HUD), premium microphones and speakers, convenient reverse control (such as steering wheel controls and knobs), and high-precision vehicle data. However, the vehicles are slow in upgrading their central control hardware's computing performance, and their apps and services are still not rich enough.

From the perspective of mobile devices, the strength list includes the up-to-date computing hardware platform capabilities (HiAI capabilities at the chipset level), software platforms, and high-speed mobile data network connectivity, as well as various popular apps and services that users have grown accustomed to.

HiCar pools resources of mobile devices and vehicles for collaboration, playing to their strengths to create smart travel experience for consumers.

#### Open Capability

HiCar leverages the distributed virtual bus technology, distributed virtualization capabilities, as well as app and service sharing and virtualization technology to build an open platform solution.

HiCar provides two important open capabilities:

     * **Open capabilities for mobile app and service access** (such as **CarBaseEngine** and **CarMapEngine**): Huawei provides open capabilities such as CarBaseEngine and CarMapEngine for mobile apps. By integrating open capabilities, mobile apps can achieve seamless transfer between phones and head units. For details, please refer to [App Integration Process](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-Guides/app-process-0000001056059155).
     * **Open capabilities for vehicle hardware device access (HiCar SDK)** : Huawei provides HiCar SDK open capabilities for vehicle hardware device vendors. By integrating HiCar SDK, vehicle hardware such as head units can quickly access HiCar and share rich application ecosystems and services of mobile phones. For details, please refer to [Device Integration Process](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-Guides/device-process-0000001052717625).

# Obtaining Documents

#### Permission Description

Currently, HUAWEI HiCar provides access documents only for development partners who are cooperating with Huawei. Partners can perform the following steps to obtain the documents.

#### Procedure

     1. Confirm the HUAWEI ID that you use to log in to the [HUAWEI Developers website](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/). Generally, the ID is an email address or mobile number.
     2. Send a HUAWEI HiCar Document Permission Application to **huaweihicar@huawei.com**.

Provide the following information in the email:

        * Company name
        * Your name
        * Your HUAWEI ID (If there are multiple IDs, collect and send them in a batch.)
        * Your current cooperation project with HiCar (specify whether the purpose is for audio app integration, map app integration, or common app integration)

After receiving the email, the HiCar team will grant you the permission to access related documents based on the project progress. Then, you can refresh this page to view related integration documents.

#### Feedback

You can click **Feedback** at the bottom of the page to inform us of any document problems or product suggestions.

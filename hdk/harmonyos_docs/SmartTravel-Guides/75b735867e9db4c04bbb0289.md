# Certification

**Figure 1** Certification process  
![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230817210215.57560414725557495181447379977100:50001231000000:2800:C60990A7F12252917C0B5058A4B7C7E56944C453C87B3C39E0AC0234FFBF767D.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

To ensure the experience of products integrated with HiCar, only HiCar products certified by Huawei can be released in the market. Huawei is entitled to technically block HiCar products released without being certified.

**Table 1** Roles and responsibilities in the certification phaseParticipant | Major Responsibilities  
---|---  
Vehicle manufacturer | Tests the products integrated with HiCar, ensures that the products pass all of the self-test cases, and provides self-test reports.  
Huawei | Reviews the HiCar self-test report provided by vehicle manufacturers, spot-checks certain test items, provides a conclusion on whether the project passes the certification, and issues certificates to certified projects in both digital and printed forms.  
  
Table 2 lists the materials that vehicle manufacturers need to provide when submitting a certification application to Huawei for the first time.

**Table 2** Materials required for certificationMaterial | Quantity | Remarks  
---|---|---  
Head unit workbench | 1 | The workbench should have complete accessories, including but not limited to the workbench box, host, screen, power supply, microphone, speaker, wheel control buttons, GPS antenna, and related wiring harness. The following figure shows an example of the head unit workbench. ![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230817210215.75972048764904103050116142780693:50001231000000:2800:DC26F34DBB0567C3297D4C78DF71A134EA6A87EB65D1EF95B4257C4B20F2692C.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)  
Vehicle | 1 | 
     * The overall software and hardware configurations of the vehicle must be the same as those for commercial use.
     * The vehicle must be in good condition and be safe to drive.
     * The vehicle must be fully charged or fueled.
     * The validity period of a temporary license is 90 days. Apply for a new temporary license if the validity period expires. The temporary license must support drive tests (ensure that the driving area that the temporary license allows includes the target city).
     * Vehicle insurance: Policies of compulsory liability insurance of vehicle traffic accidents and commercial insurance must be provided.
     * Vehicle IoT card: If a vehicle supports IoT cards, IoT cards with a traffic package of 500 MB/month or higher must be provided.
     * The inside and outside of the vehicle are clean and tidy without exposed harnesses.
     * Necessary description and explanation for vehicle conditions (such as scratches, wear, and exception alarms) should be provided.
     * Precautions during the use of the vehicle should be provided as well.

After the certification is complete, Huawei will return the vehicle and keep two head unit workbenches (one after the kick-off meeting and one in the certification phase) until two years after the vehicle model is released. (Within two years after the vehicle model is released, Huawei will use the head unit workbenches for spot-check and testing on software updates.)

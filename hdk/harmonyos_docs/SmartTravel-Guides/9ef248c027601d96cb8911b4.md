# App Release

After the app is integrated with HiCar open capabilities, the app needs to perform a self-test based on the HUAWEI HiCar test cases/acceptance report provided by Huawei before the app is released or updated to AppGallery. After the self-test is complete, the app needs to provide the version package to Huawei for re-test. After the re-test passes, the app is released to AppGallery after being agreed by two parties. After being approved by Huawei, the app will also be displayed in the HUAWEI HiCar zone.

To access the HUAWEI HiCar zone, go to **AppGallery > Apps > Featured > HUAWEI HiCar Zone**.

#### Review Guidelines

Before releasing your app, read the following for the review guidelines and FAQs you may need to know:

     * [HUAWEI AppGallery Review Guide](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/devservice/doc/50104)

     * [App Review FAQs](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/devservice/doc/50106)

#### Management Regulations on the HUAWEI HiCar Zone

**1\. Process of releasing an app in the HUAWEI HiCar zone**

     1. The app provider needs to provide the HUAWEI HiCar UI review result and HUAWEI HiCar function self-test report based on the UX review checklist and function test cases provided by Huawei. The HUAWEI HiCar team determines whether to start Huawei re-test based on the provided UI review result and self-test report.

     2. The HUAWEI HiCar team performs the re-test. If the app passes the re-test, the app provider will be notified of the app release preparation.

     3. The app provider needs to release the app of the corresponding version on AppGallery and add the content related to HUAWEI HiCar features to the app introduction.

     4. After updating the app in AppGallery, the app provider needs to notify the HUAWEI HiCar team to release the app in the HUAWEI HiCar zone.

**2\. HUAWEI HiCar zone app update**

     1. Before updating version in AppGallery, the app provider needs to notify the HUAWEI HiCar team of the UI and feature changes in the updated version and provide the corresponding self-test report for review.

     2. The HUAWEI HiCar team will evaluate and perform sampling test on the changes in the HUAWEI HiCar UI and features of the app.

     3. If the sampling test fails, the HUAWEI HiCar team will isolate the app or remove it from AppGallery.

**3\. App isolation in the HUAWEI HiCar zone**

If an app that has been released in the HUAWEI HiCar zone has critical or major issues (such as unexpected exit or crash that affect normal use), the app will be isolated:

     1. The HUAWEI HiCar team reports the problem to the app provider and isolates the app in the HUAWEI HiCar state.

     2. The app provider confirms the problem and provides the rectification plan.

     3. The app provider provides a fixed version. After the app is accepted by the HUAWEI HiCar team, the app provider updates the app to AppGallery. The HUAWEI HiCar team will de-isolate the app in the HUAWEI HiCar state.

**4\. Removing the app from the HUAWEI HiCar Zone**

     1. After an app is isolated, perform iterative repair and Huawei re-test, and update the app to AppGallery within two weeks. If the app does not meet the requirements, the HUAWEI HiCar team will remove the app from the HUAWEI HiCar zone.

     2. The app version is updated and released in AppGallery. If the HUAWEI HiCar UI and features are changed, report the changes and the corresponding self-test report to the HUAWEI HiCar team. If no feedback is provided for three consecutive times, the HUAWEI HiCar team will remove the app from the HUAWEI HiCar zone.

     3. The app must comply with the features required by HUAWEI HiCar within the specified period. If the app does not meet the requirements, the HUAWEI HiCar team will remove the app from the HUAWEI HiCar zone.

     4. If you want to release the app in the HUAWEI HiCar zone again after it is removed, follow the process of releasing the app in the HUAWEI HiCar zone.

     5. After the app is removed, it cannot participate in HUAWEI HiCar joint marketing or other activities.

#### Submitting Your App

When your app is ready for release, submit an application in [HUAWEI AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/service/josp/agc/index.html). For details, please refer to [Releasing an App](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/distribution/app/agc-release_app).

After the app is released, send the app ID that you requested from HUAWEI Developers to **huaweihicar@huawei.com** to notify the HUAWEI HiCar team for subsequent access permission configuration.

# Entry

**Suggestion 5-1: Display the HUAWEI HiCar entry on the home screen of the head unit.**

The entry can be in the form of an app icon, an information card, or the like. When HUAWEI HiCar is not connected, touching the entry will initiate a connection; when HUAWEI HiCar is connected, touching the entry will display the list of apps from the connected phone.

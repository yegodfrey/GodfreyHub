# Device Integration Process

#### Admission Application

     1. Register as a HUAWEI enterprise developer. (For details, please refer to [Registration and Verification](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/start/registration-and-verification-0000001053628148)).
     2. Download the admission application materials and sign the HUAWEI HiCar SDK Product and Service Agreement online at the first download.

After the signing, obtain the [HUAWEI HiCar Hardware Partnership Admission Application Letter](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230817210213.24243273806687487938309172879300:50001231000000:2800:1E363B1AFD2F64D59F048B0F51CC4D8A8FD2C88107D9CAACA50F7A06393BADB9.zip?needInitFileName=true) as well as its appendixes.

     3. Send the admission application materials to **huaweihicar@huawei.com**.

After receiving the application, Huawei will contact you in a timely manner and provide the admission review result.

#### Integration and Development

When obtaining Huawei's written permission, you will obtain the HUAWEI HiCar SDK software package. Complete the integration development and commissioning of the HUAWEI HiCar SDK with the help of Huawei technical support.

During integration development and commissioning, if you encounter problems or have requirements and optimization suggestions on the HiCar SDK software package or guide documents, you can:

     * Send an email with the subject of **HUAWEI HiCar SDK Requirement/Problem Feedback** to **huaweihicar@huawei.com**.
     * Submit a trouble ticket via the CEPM system (<https://workplace.cbgcpm.huawei.com/#/group/partner/portal>). If you cannot access the CEPM system, contact the HUAWEI HiCar team to obtain the permission.

#### Acceptance Test

You can officially push HiCar-related features and experience to consumers only after obtaining the HUAWEI HiCar certification.

After the integration development is complete, switch to the **Device Access** section in the navigation tree on the left of this website to view the certification test to learn about the details of obtaining the HiCar certification.

# Carrier Call (via Bluetooth)

#### Scenario

HiCar recommends that the HMI app process carrier calls through the HiCar channel. However, when HiCar processes carrier calls in virtual call mode, Bluetooth SCO connection is triggered, which may cause no sound during calls and the Bluetooth call screen being blocked. In addition, echo suppression is needed to ensure crystal clear calls.

If you have difficulties in processing echo suppression during carrier calls or no sound is heard during calls, the Bluetooth call mode can be used to process HiCar carrier calls.

#### Development Procedure

When a carrier call is initiated, the mobile phone sends the call data to the head unit through the Bluetooth HFP channel. You can process the call according to the Bluetooth HFP call process.

If you have disabled HFP mode for issue workaround after HiCar is successfully connected, perform the following operations to adjust:

     1. After HiCar is successfully connected, disconnect the A2DP instead of the HFP. In addition, when HiCar is connected, ensure that the Bluetooth call or incoming call screen of the head unit does not block the HMI app screen that integrates HiCar.
     2. After HiCar is disconnected, restore the A2DP connection.

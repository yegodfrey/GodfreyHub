# Converged Display of Apps

**Rule 5-1: When the head unit is connected to HUAWEI HiCar, all apps supporting HUAWEI HiCar from the phone are displayed on the head unit together with native head unit apps.**

Apps from the phone are sorted by the head unit system, and converge on the head unit in the following forms:

#### Form 1: Displayed in the HUAWEI HiCar Tab

It is recommended that a tab named "HUAWEI HiCar" be added next to the native head unit app center, to display apps from the phone.

**Rule 5-2: The HUAWEI HiCar Smart Desktop app must be displayed on top of others in the tab.**

For details about operation interactions of HUAWEI HiCar Smart Desktop, see [HUAWEI HiCar Smart Desktop](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-Guides/app-fusion-0000001504669890#section124617421795).

#### Form 2: Displayed Together with Native Head Unit Apps

**Rule 5-3: The HUAWEI HiCar Smart Desktop app must be displayed on the first screen in the native head unit app center.**

**Rule 5-4: Apps from the phone should be displayed with "HUAWEI HiCar" following their names, for users to distinguish the app source.**

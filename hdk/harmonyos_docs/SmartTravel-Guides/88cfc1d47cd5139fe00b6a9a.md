# About This Document

#### Design Objectives

HUAWEI HiCar is a solution that Huawei provides to empower users with smart features in all scenarios, and every conceivable environment – in a vehicle, at house, or on the go. HUAWEI HiCar connects mobile phones to vehicles to pool their capabilities for mutual reinforcement, creating a next-level, phone-centric smart travel experience for users.

The design objectives include ensuring driving safety, enabling convenient and comfortable operations, and providing smart and considerate services.

#### HUAWEI HiCar System Layout

The HUAWEI HiCar UI consists of the dock area, system information area, and home screen.

     * **Dock area** : Provides entries to common functions, such as voice assistant, navigation, communications, music, and home screen.
     * **System information area** : Displays phone status information, such as signal strength, battery level, and time.
     * **Content area** : Displays the home screen (in the form of cards or app icons) and app content.

#### HUAWEI HiCar System Home Screen

The home screen consists of the card screen and app icon screen. You can swipe up and down or tap the icon in the dock area to switch between the two screens. By default, the card screen is displayed.

**Figure 3** Home screen  
![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230817210216.02002181023625730803353933837265:50001231000000:2800:BED86AA7787CDC935322C858577276E31C9002A7B363CD96BDF912DBF1DC63A2.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

The card screen consists of cards of running apps and services. Service information that meets system requirements can also be pushed to the home screen through cards, allowing users to quickly obtain scenario-based services. The cards provide quick access to apps or core functions of apps.

#### Switching to the Native Head Unit System from HUAWEI HiCar

**Rule 1-1: Keep the HUAWEI HiCar connection when returning to the native UI of the head unit from HUAWEI HiCar.**

On the app icon screen of HUAWEI HiCar, the user can touch the icon of the native head unit system to return to the native UI.

#### HUAWEI HiCar Converged Desktop

The converged desktop is an innovative form for HUAWEI HiCar to further integrate into the native head unit system. Apps and content from mobile phones can be seamlessly integrated into the head unit to build the next-level interconnection experience.

For details about the design specifications of the converged desktop, see the [HUAWEI HiCar Converged Desktop](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-Guides/entry-fusion-0000001552335177).

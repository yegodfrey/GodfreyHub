# Background Control

**Rule 2-5: Use the logo in the standard color when the background brightness is greater than or equal to 70%, and ensure enough color contrast between the background image and logo.**

Ensure that there is sufficient color contrast between the background image and the logo when the logo is placed against an image. Avoid placing the logo against a complex image to ensure legibility and presence of the logo.

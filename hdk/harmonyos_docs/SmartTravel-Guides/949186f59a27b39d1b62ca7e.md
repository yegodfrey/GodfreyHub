# Responsibilities and Obligations  
  
#### Vehicle Manufacturer

1\. Vehicle manufacturers shall ensure that they do not maliciously crack Huawei HiCar functions, such as reverse engineering.

2\. Vehicle manufacturers shall not abuse HUAWEI HiCar to infringe users' personal information, user privacy, national information security, or other activities that violate cyber security laws.

3\. Vehicle manufacturers shall comply with the non-disclosure agreement signed with Huawei.

#### Huawei

1\. Huawei shall ensure that the development of HUAWEI HiCar functions complies with the development and test management requirements formulated by Huawei Device.

2\. Huawei shall ensure that the function code of HUAWEI HiCar passes the code security check specified by Huawei Device.

3\. Huawei shall comply with the non-disclosure agreement signed with vehicle manufacturers.

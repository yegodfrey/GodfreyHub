# Others  
  
#### Joint Release

After devices integrated with HiCar are certified and approved by Huawei for release, marketing teams of both parties can cooperate for joint marketing or release if necessary.

#### Lifecycle Maintenance

After the product is released, the solution for dealing with negative public opinions, issues, and software updates are as follows:

     * Mobile app issues will be resolved through HarmonyOS updates, which are pushed on a regular basis. Special major issues are processed individually.
     * Issues about the head unit ROM and the SDK will be resolved through SDK and ROM updates. Huawei provides the updated SDK to vehicle manufacturers as scheduled, and vehicle manufacturers push the updated ROM and update schedules as required.

# About the Cooperation

This Proposal provides suggestions on the cooperation between vehicle manufacturers and Huawei in pre-installing HUAWEI HiCar (HiCar for short) on head units.

To improve the efficiency and quality of the cooperation, vehicle manufacturers should read the Proposal carefully before the cooperation, get familiar with the relevant processes and requirements, and configure teams and personnel according to the Proposal.

Vehicle manufacturers who have any questions or suggestions on this Proposal can discuss with the HiCar team, to reach an agreed conclusion for further cooperation.

#### Responsibilities of Both Parties

**Table 1** Participants and their responsibilitiesParticipant | Major Responsibilities  
---|---  
Vehicle manufacturer | Develops an overall project plan, keeps track of the progress, coordinates relevant resources, and completes HiCar integration, development, verification, and subsequent maintenance for the head unit.  
Huawei | Reviews HiCar projects, provides the HiCar SDK, integration documents, and technical support, and makes the certification conclusion.  
  
#### Scope of Cooperation

The following table lists the cooperation scope between vehicle manufacturers and Huawei on HiCar.

**Table 2** Scope of cooperationScope | Content  
---|---  
**HiCar requirement determining and technical evaluation** | Before official cooperation, Huawei and vehicle manufacturers should determine the requirements and complete technical evaluations. HiCar functions on the head unit are classified into mandatory functions and optional functions. Mandatory functions must be implemented by vehicle manufacturers during integration and development. Vehicle manufacturers without implementing mandatory functions will not pass Huawei's certification. Optional functions can be implemented as required based on communications between Huawei and vehicle manufacturers. Mandatory functions include wireless connection, automatic reconnection, and starting HMI apps via the phone's Bluetooth (HiCar will be recommended to users for Bluetooth connection); optional functions include Internet sharing, music/phone/navigation metadata display, and head unit camera integration (using the head unit camera to make video calls). In addition, vehicle manufacturers can feel free to discuss with Huawei about any new features. After determining the HiCar-related requirements, Huawei will review the software and hardware capabilities of the head unit and evaluate whether the head unit meets the HiCar admission standards.  
**HiCar integration and development** | Huawei cooperates with vehicle manufacturers to complete the integration, development, and pre-installation of HiCar on the target vehicle models/platforms, in order to provide HiCar services for consumers.  
**HiCar device certification** | After completing the HiCar integration and development on the head unit, vehicle manufacturers complete the test 100% according to the test cases provided by Huawei and provide the test report. Huawei reviews the test report, spot-checks related test items, and provides a certification conclusion. Vehicle manufacturers who pass the certification will receive certificates in both digital and printed forms.  
**Joint HiCar marketing** | Huawei and vehicle manufacturers jointly initiate marketing campaigns about the target vehicle models/platforms supporting HiCar through their individual channels.  
**HiCar operations & maintenance** | Within the lifecycle of the target vehicle models/platforms, Huawei and vehicle manufacturers are both responsible for the updates and iteration of the head unit system/HiCar, and jointly resolve possible HiCar-related issues/negative public opinions.  
  
This document provides suggestions on the cooperation between Huawei and vehicle manufacturers, and particularly focuses on determining HiCar requirements, technical evaluation, integration, development, and device certification. Contents about joint HiCar operations & maintenance are provided separately. Vehicle manufacturers can learn about these contents after completing the HiCar integration and obtaining the certification.

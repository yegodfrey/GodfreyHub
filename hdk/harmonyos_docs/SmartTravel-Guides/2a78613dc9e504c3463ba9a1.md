# Overview

#### Wireless Connection Process

Initial connection: After the head unit initiates a wireless connection, the user confirms the connection on the phone and enters the connection code. The HUAWEI HiCar screen is then displayed on the head unit.

Non-initial connection: When the phone approaches the car, the head unit will automatically enter the HUAWEI HiCar screen (when automatic connection is enabled and the user did not manually disconnect HUAWEI HiCar at last connection).

#### Wired Connection Process

Initial connection: Connect the phone to the car using a USB cable. After the connection is confirmed and authorized on the phone, the HUAWEI HiCar screen is displayed on the head unit.

Non-initial connection: After the phone is connected to the car via USB, the head unit will automatically enter the HUAWEI HiCar screen (when automatic connection is enabled).

# Admission Evaluation  
  
Huawei will evaluate the project feasibility from aspects of resources, technologies, and organizations to verify that the project can be initiated.

     1. Vehicle manufacturers apply for admission following the "Device Integration Process" on the left side of the website.

During the application, vehicle manufacturers will obtain relevant files including _HUAWEI HiCar Software and Hardware Resource Constraints_ and _HUAWEI HiCar Product Plan & Communication Matrix_. Fill in the files and apply for admission based on the device integration process.

     2. After receiving the application, Huawei will clarify the requirements of the HiCar integration project with the vehicle manufacturer. To be specific:
        * Clarifies any questions and determines the project specifications.
        * Reviews the _HUAWEI HiCar Software and Hardware Resource Constraints_ and evaluates whether the head unit meets the HiCar admission standards.

The following table lists the key roles involved in requirement clarification.

**Table 1** Key rolesParticipant | Key Role | Responsibility  
---|---|---  
Vehicle manufacturer | HiCar project manager | 
        * Specifies the business value of initiating the project, and evaluates the project feasibility from aspects of organizations, resources, and technical readiness.
        * Evaluates whether the head unit meets the requirements for integrating HiCar according to the _HUAWEI HiCar Software and Hardware Resource Constraints_ provided by Huawei.  
Huawei | HiCar technical support | Considers the technical feasibility of the project from a professional perspective, and pays special attention to whether the software and hardware configurations of the head unit meet the HUAWEI HiCar admission standards.

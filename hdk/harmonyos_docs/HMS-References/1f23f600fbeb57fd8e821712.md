com.huawei.hms.mlsdk.document.MLDocument.Word              
用来描述检测出的词，继承自Base，包含6个属性：1、[getLanguageList](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：检测出词的语种；2、[getInterval](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：词的间隔类型；3、[getBorder](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：词的边框；4、[getStringValue](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：词的内容；5、[getPossibility](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：检测结果置信度；6、getCharacterList()：词中包含的所有字符。

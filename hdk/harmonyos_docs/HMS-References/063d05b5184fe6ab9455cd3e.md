**public final class CameraUpdate**  
  
CameraUpdate定义了相机移动参数。通过调用[animateCamera](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/hms-map-huaweimap#animateCamera\(CameraUpdate\))(CameraUpdate)或者[moveCamera](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/hms-map-huaweimap#moveCamera\(CameraUpdate\))(CameraUpdate)等可以移动地图相机，开发者可以通过工厂类[CameraUpdateFactory](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/hms-map-cameraupdatef)获取CameraUpdate对象，具体使用方法参考[CameraUpdateFactory](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/hms-map-cameraupdatef)。

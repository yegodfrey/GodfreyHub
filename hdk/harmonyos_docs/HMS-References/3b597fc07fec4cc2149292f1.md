com.huawei.hms.mlsdk.document.MLDocument.Character  
用来描述检测出的单一字符，是模型能识别的最小元素，包含继承自Base的5个属性：1、[getLanguageList](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：检测出字符的语种；2、[getInterval](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：字符的间隔类型；3、[getBorder](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：字符的边框；4、[getStringValue](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：字符内容；5、[getPossibility](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMS-References/MLDocumenBase-4)()：检测结果置信度。

|序号|任务|说明|
|:-|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|1|[创建项目与应用](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-storage-createprojectandapp-0000001275170098)|项目是您在AppGallery Connect（以下简称AGC）资源的组织实体，您可以将一个应用的不同平台版本添加到同一个项目中。 > 说明 > 您可以通过创建不同的项目，实现分别在测试环境和开发环境使用云存储。|
|2|[开通云存储服务](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-enable-service-0000001275330014)|-|
|3|[在IDE中创建快应用项目](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-storage-quickapp-ide-0000001334172817)|在IDE开发环境中创建快应用项目，并与在AGC上创建的快应用关联。|
|4|[集成SDK](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-sdk-quickapp-0000001072733544)|在IDE开发环境中集成AGC JS SDK及云存储JS SDK。|
|5|[初始化存储实例](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-storage-initialize-bucket-quickapp-0000001281453000)|在使用云存储功能前，需初始化存储实例。|
|6|管理文件|通过调用云存储JS SDK的API，您可以[创建引用](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-createreference-quickapp-0000001072107653)，通过文件的引用进行[上传文件](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-upload-quickapp-0000001072255650)、[删除文件](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-delete-quickapp-0000001072333611)、[文件元数据管理](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-metadata-quickapp-0000001071965502)及[获取文件的下载地址](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-download-quickapp-0000001071925361)等操作，或者通过创建目录的引用来[获取该目录下的文件列表](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-cloudstorage-list-quickapp-0000001072005417)。|

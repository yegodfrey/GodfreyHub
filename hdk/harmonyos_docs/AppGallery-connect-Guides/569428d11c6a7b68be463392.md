|5|根据业务需要，实现不同账号的登录认证。 * [手机](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-harmonyos-arkts-login-phone-0000001631566338) * [邮箱](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-harmonyos-arkts-login-email-0000001679886157) * [匿名账号](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-harmonyos-arkts-login-anonymous-0000001633997272) * [关联账号](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-harmonyos-arkts-login-linkaccount-0000001680046005)|-|

|7|[销户](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-auth-harmonyos-arkts-deregistration-0000001631406394)|当用户需要注销当前账号时，可以进行销户。|

|序号|任务|说明|
|:-|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------|
|1|[创建项目与应用](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-create-project-and-app-0000001275891238)|项目是您在AGC资源的组织实体，您可以将一个应用的不同平台版本添加到同一个项目中。|
|2|[开通服务](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-enable-service-0000001276051134)|-|
|3|[集成SDK](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-miniprogram-integration-sdk-0000001194308625)|在使用远程配置相关功能前，您需要将AGC SDK及远程配置SDK集成到您的开发环境。|
|4|[初始化远程配置](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-miniprogram-initialization-config-0000001334777045)|加载最近一次成功获取云端配置信息时间、上一次获取云端配置信息是否成功以及您设置的默认配置信息。|
|5|[设置应用内默认值](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-miniprogram-setvalue-0000001334617553)|您可以在远程配置对象中设置应用程序内的默认值，以便您的应用程序在连接到远程配置服务之前按照预期运行。|
|6|[设置云端配置项](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-miniprogram-cloudconfig-0000001194348737)|您可以在AGC创建新的云端配置项，根据所需的条件逻辑，将新的配置项替换到应用中。|
|7|[获取云端配置项](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/AppGallery-connect-Guides/agc-remoteconfig-miniprogram-obtainconfig-0000001148229038)|您可以通过相关接口获取所需配置项的值。|

# DMSDPAudioStreamType  
  
Enumerator | Description  
---|---  
AUDIO_STREAM_VOICE_CALL = 0 | Audio stream of a phone call.  
AUDIO_STREAM_SYSTEM = 1 | Audio stream of the system sound.  
AUDIO_STREAM_RING = 2 | Audio stream of a ringtone.  
AUDIO_STREAM_MUSIC = 3 | Audio stream of music playback.  
AUDIO_STREAM_ALARM = 4 | Audio stream of an alarm.  
AUDIO_STREAM_NOTIFICATION = 5 | Audio stream of a notification.  
AUDIO_STREAM_BLUETOOTH_SCO = 6 | Audio stream for a Bluetooth voice call.  
AUDIO_STREAM_SYSTEM_ENFORCED = 7 | Audio stream of the enforced system sound.  
AUDIO_STREAM_DTMF = 8 | Audio stream generated when a key is pressed during a call.  
AUDIO_STREAM_TTS = 9 | Audio stream of text-to-speech (TTS).  
AUDIO_STREAM_ACCESSIBILITY = 10 | Audio stream of an accessibility feature.

# TrustPhoneInfo  
  
Name | Type | Description  
---|---|---  
phoneIdRealLen | uint32_t | Length of the phone ID.  
phoneId[PHONE_ID_MAX_LEN] | uint8_t | Unique phone identifier.  
phoneNameRealLen | uint32_t | Length of the phone name.  
phoneName[PHONE_NAME_MAX_LEN] | uint8_t | Phone name.  
phoneBrMacRealLen | uint32_t | Length of the Bluetooth address of the phone.  
phoneBrMac[BR_MAC_MAX_LEN] | uint8_t | Bluetooth address of the phone.  
lastConnectTime | int64_t | System time of the last connection.  
connectStatus | uint32_t | Connection status. For details, please refer to [CarConnectionStatus](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-References/car-connection-status-0000001053502171).

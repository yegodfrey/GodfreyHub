# Definition

Name | Description  
---|---  
HICAR_HANDLE void* | HiCar handle, which is returned after the internal initialization of the HiCar SDK is complete.  
PHONE_ID_MAX_LEN 256 | Maximum length of **phoneId** in the **TrustPhoneInfo** structure.  
PHONE_NAME_MAX_LEN 128 | Maximum length of **phoneName** in the **TrustPhoneInfo** structure.  
PHONE_TRUST_MAX_LIST 16 | Maximum number of **TrustPhoneInfo** records that can be obtained.  
MODEL_ID_MAX_LEN 8 | Maximum length of **modelId** in the **CarConnectInfo** structure.  
BR_MAC_MAX_LEN 20 | Maximum length of **brMac** in the **CarConnectInfo** structure.  
CAR_DAY_NIGHT_MAX_LEN 12 | Maximum length of **dayNightInfo** in the **CarDeviceInfo** structure.  
CAR_BRAND_MAX_LEN 64 | Maximum length of **carBrand** in the **CarDeviceInfo** structure.

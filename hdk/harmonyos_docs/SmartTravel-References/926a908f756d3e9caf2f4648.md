# HwRemoteCtrlEventDirection  
  
Enumerator | Description  
---|---  
DIRECTION_FROM_SOURCE | Sending from the phone to the head unit.  
DIRECTION_FROM_SINK | Sending from the head unit to the phone.  
DIRECTION_UNKNOWN | The direction of reverse control is unknown.

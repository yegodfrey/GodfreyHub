# GetAudioCapability  
  
Function  
---  
int32_t (*GetAudioCapability)(DMSDPAudioCapabilities** capabilities, uint32_t *num)  
Obtains the attributes of available audio devices (including speakers and microphones) in the form of specified structure.  
  
**Parameters**

**Name** | **Type** | **Description**  
---|---|---  
** capabilities | [DMSDPAudioCapabilities](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-References/dmsdp-audio-capabilities-0000001051490470) | Audio capability set.  
*num | uint32_t | Number of capability sets, including speakers and microphones.  
  
**Return**

Type | Description  
---|---  
int32_t | Attributes of available audio devices (including speakers and microphones).

# DMSDPAudioDevice  
  
Enumerator | Description  
---|---  
AUDIO_DEVICE_TYPE_SPEAKER = 0u | Virtual speaker, which is used for music playback, navigation guidance, or other purposes.  
AUDIO_DEVICE_TYPE_MIC = 1u | Virtual microphone that is used to collect voice during voice interaction.  
AUDIO_DEVICE_TYPE_MODEM_SPEAKER = 2u | Virtual speaker that is used to play voice of the other party during a call.  
AUDIO_DEVICE_TYPE_MODEM_MIC = 3u | Virtual microphone that is used to collect voice of the local party during a call.

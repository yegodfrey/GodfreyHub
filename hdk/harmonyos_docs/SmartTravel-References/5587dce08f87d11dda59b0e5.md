# DMSDPAudioType  
  
Enumerator | Description  
---|---  
AUDIO_USAGE_UNKNOWN = 0 | Unknown audio usage.  
AUDIO_USAGE_MEDIA = 1 | Audio for media usage, such as music or audio tracks in movies.  
AUDIO_USAGE_VOICE_COMMUNICATION = 2 | Audio for phone call usage, such as carrier or VoIP calls.  
AUDIO_USAGE_VOICE_COMMUNICATION_SIGNALLING = 3 | Signal tone during a call, such as a busy tone or a DTMF key tone.  
AUDIO_USAGE_ALARM = 4 | Audio for alarms, such as alarm ringtones.  
AUDIO_USAGE_NOTIFICATION = 5 | Audio for notifications.  
AUDIO_USAGE_NOTIFICATION_TELEPHONY_RINGTONE = 6 | Audio for phone ringtones.  
AUDIO_USAGE_NOTIFICATION_COMMUNICATION_REQUEST = 7 | Prompt tone for when a user requests to join or end a call, for example, joining a VoIP or video conference.  
AUDIO_USAGE_NOTIFICATION_COMMUNICATION_INSTANT = 8 | Audio for instant messaging notifications, such as the SMS notification tone.  
AUDIO_USAGE_NOTIFICATION_COMMUNICATION_DELAYED = 9 | Audio for notifications of non-instantaneous methods of communication, such as email notifications.  
AUDIO_USAGE_NOTIFICATION_EVENT = 10 | Alert tone, such as the low battery alert tone.  
AUDIO_USAGE_ASSISTANCE_ACCESSIBILITY = 11 | Audio for an accessibility feature, such as screen reader sounds.  
AUDIO_USAGE_ASSISTANCE_NAVIGATION_GUIDANCE = 12 | Navigation voice guidance.  
AUDIO_USAGE_ASSISTANCE_SONIFICATION = 13 | Audio for user interaction.  
AUDIO_USAGE_GAME = 14 | Game sounds.  
AUDIO_USAGE_VIRTUAL_SOURCE = 15 | Sound of a virtual audio source.  
AUDIO_USAGE_ASSISTANT = 16 | Audio that responds to user queries; explanatory sounds.  
AUDIO_USAGE_TTS = 17 | TTS audio.

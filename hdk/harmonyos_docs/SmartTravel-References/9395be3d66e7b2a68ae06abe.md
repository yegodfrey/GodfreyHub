# DMSDPAudioCapabilities  
  
Name | Type | Description  
---|---|---  
*id | char | Audio device ID, such as **mic1** or **speaker1**.  
idLen | uint32_t | Length of the audio device ID.  
type | [DMSDPAudioDevice](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-References/dmsdp-audio-device-0000001053089211) | Audio device type.  
profiles | [DMSDPAudioProfiles](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-References/dmsdp-audio-profiles-0000001052249190) | Audio profiles supported by the audio device, such as the sampling rate and number of channels.  
codecs | [DMSDPAudioCodecs](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-References/dmsdp-audio-codecs-0000001052570753) | Codec supported by the audio device, such as PCM and AAC.  
reserveds | [DMSDPReserveds](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/SmartTravel-References/dmsdp-reserveds-0000001052570505) | Reserved field for future extension. For example, whether the speaker supports audio distribution.

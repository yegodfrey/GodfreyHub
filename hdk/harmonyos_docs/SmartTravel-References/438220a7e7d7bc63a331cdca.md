# GPSLocation  
  
Function  
---  
void (*GPSLocation)(char *location, int32_t len)  
Reports location information, including the longitude, latitude, height, precision, satellite time, and moving direction. The reporting starts when **RegisterGpsDataCallBack** is called and continues until **UnRegisterGpsDataCallBack** is called.  
  
**Parameters**

**Name** | **Type** | Description  
---|---|---  
*location | char | Specific location. Example: $GPGGA,092204.999,4250.5589,S,14718.5084,E,1,04,24.4,12.2,M,19.7,M,,0000*1F$GPRMC,024813.640,A,3158.4608,N,11848.3737,E,10.05,324.27,150706,,,A*50  
len | int32_t | Data length.

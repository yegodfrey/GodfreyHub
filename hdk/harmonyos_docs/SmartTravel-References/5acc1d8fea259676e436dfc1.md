# startBluetoothRecommend  
  
Function  
---  
startBluetoothRecommend  
Recommends the use of HiCar after the HMI app detects a Bluetooth connection. After receiving the feedback from the mobile phone, the HMI app is notified through the **onShowStartPage** callback function of **CarListener**.

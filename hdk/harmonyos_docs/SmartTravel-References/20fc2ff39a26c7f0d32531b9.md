# CarDataType  
  
Enumerator | Description  
---|---  
HICAR_DATA_UNKNOWN=0 | Unknown data type.  
HICAR_DATA_VEHICLE_CONTROL=500 | Control of the vehicle air conditioner, windows, and sunroof.  
HICAR_DATA_DAY_NIGHT=501 | Dark/Light Mode  
HICAR_DATA_BRAND_ICON_DATA=502 | Vehicle brand icon.  
HICAR_DATA_NAV_FOCUS=503 | Navigation focus.  
HICAR_DATA_CALL_STATE_FOCUS=504 | Phone status and focus.  
HICAR_DATA_VOICE_STATE=505 | Voice state.  
HICAR_DATA_DRIVING_MODE=506 | Driving mode.  
HICAR_DATA_CAR_STATE= 507 | Vehicle body data.  
HICAR_DATA_SERVICE_CHANNEL=508 | Vehicle service channel.  
HICAR_DATA_KEYCODE=509 | Physical shortcut keys on the head unit.  
HICAR_DATA_SENSOR_DATA=510 | Vehicle sensor data.  
HICAR_DATA_NET_SERVICE=511 | Network service.  
HICAR_DATA_BLUETOOTH=512 | Bluetooth service.  
HICAR_DATA_SCREEN_SERVICE=513 | Screen management service.  
HICAR_DATA_AA_DATA=514 | App metadata (applicable to limited HiCar app versions).  
HICAR_DATA_AA_MEDIA_DATA=515 | Media metadata (applicable to limited HiCar app versions).  
HICAR_DATA_AA_CALL_DATA=516 | Call metadata (applicable to limited HiCar app versions).  
HICAR_DATA_AA_NAVIGATION_DATA=517 | Navigation metadata (applicable to limited HiCar app versions).  
HICAR_DATA_MANUAL_DISCONNECT=518 | Message for manually disconnecting HiCar (applicable to limited HiCar app versions).  
HICAR_DATA_VOICE_PROMPT=519 | Local prompt tones on the head unit (applicable to limited HiCar app versions).

# onBinderDied  
  
Function  
---  
void onBinderDied()  
The **onBinderDied** method is used to trigger callback when a service exception occurs. For example, the service exits abnormally or is killed by another process. The upper-layer app can restart the service or clear the resources (by clearing the **HiCarAdapter** instantiation object) based on the service scenario requirements.

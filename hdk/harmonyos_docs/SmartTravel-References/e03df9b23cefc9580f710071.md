# getCustomizedAudioAttributes (Beta)  
  
Function  
---  
CustomizedAudioAttributes getCustomizedAudioAttributes(int[] usage, int[] contentType, int focusGain, Map<String, String> info)  
The HMI app constructs **AudioAttributes** or **StreamType** based on the original information mapping and returns **AudioAttributes** or **StreamType** to the HiCar SDK via callback. The SDK then applies for the focus based on the mapped **AudioAttributes** or **StreamType** to manage the focus or creates an **AudioTrack** to play audio streams.  
  
**Parameters**

**Parameter Name** | **Parameter Type** | **Parameter Description**  
---|---|---  
usage | int[] | Usage of the audio data sent by the phone. In the multi-audio source mixing scenario, there are multiple valid **usage** values. All valid **usage** values of the audio data are reported to the head unit app, and the HMI app determines the priority and conversion policy.  
contentType | int[] | Similar to the usage policy, all **contentType** parameters of the audio data are reported to the head unit app.  
focusGain | int | Audio focus type. For details, see the audio focus parameters of Google AudioManager. Example: **0** : Non-Google-AudioManager audio focus parameter. This parameter is newly added to distinguish whether the API is called to play audio or apply for the audio focus. **1** : Permanent focus, such as music. **2** : Transient focus, such as the call, notification, alarm, and instant message app. **3** : Duck focus, such as navigation. **4** : Exclusive and transient focus. If the value is **0** , the **streamtype** or **AudioAttributes** required for audio playback is being obtained. The HMI app needs to construct **streamtype** or **AudioAttributes** according to **usage** in the passed parameters. Based on the constructed **streamtype** or **AudioAttributes** , instantiate and return **CustomizedAudioAttributes**. If the value is not **0** , the **streamtype** or **AudioAttributes** required for audio focus application is being obtained. For details about the passed focus type, see the audio focus parameter of Google AudioManager. The HMI app needs to construct **streamtype** or **AudioAttributes** according to the **usage** , or according to both **usage** and the value of **focusGain**. Based on the constructed **streamtype** or **AudioAttributes** , instantiate and return **CustomizedAudioAttributes**.  
info | Map<String, String> | Reserved extended attribute, which is used to report the audio information on the phone to the head unit app.  
  
**Return**

Type | Description  
---|---  
CustomizedAudioAttributes | Attributes of a customized audio policy.

# OH_AbilityRuntime_ConnectOptions

> phone 26.0.0+ | 2in1 26.0.0+ | tablet 26.0.0+ | tv 26.0.0+ | wearable 26.0.0+

typedef struct OH_AbilityRuntime_ConnectOptions OH_AbilityRuntime_ConnectOptions

## 概述

定义OH_AbilityRuntime_ConnectOptions结构体类型，用于配置连接ExtensionAbility时的回调选项，包括连接成功、断开连接和连接失败的回调。

**起始版本：** 26.0.0

**相关模块：** [AbilityRuntime](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/capi-abilityruntime)

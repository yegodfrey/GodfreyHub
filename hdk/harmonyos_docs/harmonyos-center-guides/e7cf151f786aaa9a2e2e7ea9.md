rcp请求是否有数据大小限制 rcp请求默认情况下，[response](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/remote-communication-rcp#response)响应中最大数据量为50MB，超过此限制建议通过[HttpEventsHandler](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/remote-communication-rcp#httpeventshandler)的[onDataReceive](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/remote-communication-rcp#ondatareceive)实现流式数据接收。 **参考链接** [response](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/remote-communication-rcp#response) [HttpEventsHandler](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/remote-communication-rcp#httpeventshandler) [onDataReceive](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/harmonyos-references/remote-communication-rcp#ondatareceive)

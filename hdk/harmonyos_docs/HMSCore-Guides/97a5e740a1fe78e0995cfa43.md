https://health-api.cloud.huawei.com/healthkit/v2/activityRecords/{activityRecordId}

## 读取骑行活动数据

通过ActivityRecord读取用户已创建的运动记录列表，activityType值为13表示骑行活动，用户可以设置查询条件，如活动开始时间、结束时间、活动类型、关联原子数据类型等。

**HTTP请求** **，** 请参见[查询运动记录](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/activityrecords_list-0000001050114862#section984123664716)接口

```screen
GET 
https://health-api.cloud.huawei.com/healthkit/v2/activityRecords?startTime=***&endTime=***&activityType=***&detailDataType=***
```

**请求示例**

```screen
GET 
https://health-api.cloud.huawei.com/healthkit/v2/activityRecords?startTime=1654501318285&endTime=1654501918285&activityType=13
&detailDataType=com.huawei.instantaneous.location.sample&detailDataType=com.huawei.instantaneous.speed&detailDataType=com.huawei.instantaneous.altitude&detailDataType=com.huawei.instantaneous.exercise_heart_rate
```

**请求头**

```screen
Content-Type: application/json
Authorization: Bearer ***
x-client-id: ***
x-version: ***
x-caller-trace-id: ***
```

#### MAC环境下HTTPS域名证书获取方式

                1. 点击 chrome浏览器左侧小锁图标，然后点击“证书”。

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230926115337.50690041608938620755748446489927:50001231000000:2800:A13797F4294951D958B0F09119A06D2788FC9B733732E394182906829C07EE8B.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

                2. 点击“好”，然后点击MAC设备中“启动台 > 钥匙串”，右上角搜索“huawei”得到如下界面，点击导出“*.huawei.com”。

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230926115338.54620803210222508874731840584721:50001231000000:2800:36E8F58226769533D47396FA5D1C5B7913B9AA9A0E758D991F548003BA7C6557.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230926115338.07827623441690851829238035803198:50001231000000:2800:9730FDFCC17BC10356CD90E3CA4D9FDFE42A2F12898E48F5AEFF1EDAEA5D0120.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

                3. 选择需要储存的位置，再选择“增强保密邮件（.pem）”格式，然后点击“存储”。

![](https://communityfile-drcn.op.dbankcloud.cn/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20230926115338.53046770628269309462991372084523:50001231000000:2800:B027B8C39D99AF4DCB24F3AA0988A10E86BEBEB1A029FA1D395CBF0687962ED2.png?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true?needInitFileName=true)

                4. 选择您存储的位置查看，将导出的证书用文本编辑器打开后复制即可。

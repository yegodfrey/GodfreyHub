# 版本更新说明

## 6.7.0.300 （2022-08-30）

|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Android版本：com.huawei.hihealth:hihealthkit:6.7.0.300 **新增特性** * 新增支持自由训练运动的[开始](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/extended-starting-ending-workout-0000001052835041#section171644221397)、[暂停](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/extended-starting-ending-workout-0000001052835041#section14119195383419)、[恢复](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/extended-starting-ending-workout-0000001052835041#section19802201513811)、[结束](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/extended-starting-ending-workout-0000001052835041#section4741161014109)功能。 * 新增[对设备下发控制指令](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/extended-starting-ending-workout-0000001052835041#section148961144122012)调节阻力。 修改特性 * 预置<queries>配置，以实现您的应用在Android11上或更高系统上运行时，正常调用运动健康App。您的开发环境必须满足[Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) 3.6.1及以上版本、Android Gradle插件 3.5.4及以上。|

> 说明
>
> 匹配运动健康App 12.1.8.300及以上版本。

|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Android版本：com.huawei.hihealth:hihealthkit:5.0.0.500 **修改特性** 新增接口[registerSportData](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/hihealthdatastore-0000001071549459#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002547961909__registerSportData-android_content_Context-com_huawei_hihealthkit_data_store_HiSportDataCallback-)、[unregisterSportData](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/hihealthdatastore-0000001071549459#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002547961909__unregisterSportData-android_content_Context-com_huawei_hihealthkit_data_store_HiSportDataCallback-)分别替代startRealTimeSportData及stopRealTimeSportData接口（已废弃）。 **新增特性** * 新增查询体温数据、有氧四件套（通过华为认证的跑步机、椭圆机、划船机、动感单车）实时运动数据、普通睡眠数据。 * 新增开始和结束运动2个接口[startSport](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/hihealthdatastore-0000001071549459#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002547961909__startSport-android_content_Context-int-com_huawei_hihealth_listener_ResultCallback-)/[stopSport](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/hihealthdatastore-0000001071549459#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002547961909__stopSport-android_content_Context-com_huawei_hihealth_listener_ResultCallback-)，开发者可以让用户直接在本应用上启动运动或结束运动，而无需跳转到华为运动健康App运动界面进行操作。目前支持四种运动类型：户外步行、户外跑步、户外骑行、室内跑步。 * 新增查询用户授权结果接口[getDataAuthStatusEx](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-References/hihealthauth-0000001183790426#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002547961925__getDataAuthStatusEx-android_content_Context-int-A-int-A-com_huawei_hihealthkit_auth_IDataAuthStatusListener-)，开发者可以使用此接口获取用户是否授予相关权限。 * 优化[错误码](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/HMSCore-Guides/extended-errocode-0000001053256958)。|

# 使用入门

#### 开发流程

您需要按照如下流程完成应用的开发工作。

序号 | 步骤 | 说明  
---|---|---  
1 | [开发准备](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358) | 
      * 在开发应用前，需要完成步骤：[注册成为开发者](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section47264296)、[创建应用](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section294363472920)、[开启天气服务权限](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section2119133716579)、[获取凭据](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section169441820428)、[获取token](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section639417491737)。
      * 如果您已经在[AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/service/josp/agc/index.html)网站创建了应用，可[获取凭据](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section169441820428)之后进行[获取token](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/preparations-0000001211350358#section639417491737)。  
2 | 套餐选择 | 参见[表1](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/app-dev-0000001211354538#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000001211354538__table1872612277393)。  
3 | [天气类API](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/app-dev-0000001211354538#section92478493161) | 根据城市查询天气实况信息、逐日预报、逐小时预报。  
[空气类API](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides/app-dev-0000001211354538#section16832172713195) | 根据城市查询空气质量数据，实况AQI、逐日AQI预报、逐小时AQI预报。

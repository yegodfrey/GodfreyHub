|Struct Info|
|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Struct HwArAnchor 锚点对象，引用类型。代表与可跟踪对象关联的空间位置。 * 可使用[HwArSession_acquireNewAnchor](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_session-0000001060493634#section11547163453310)、[HwArHitResult_acquireNewAnchor](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_hitresult-0000001060971426#section11547163453310)方法创建新锚点。 * 可使用[HwArAnchor_detach](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_anchor-0000001060493630#section1746616417331)使AR Engine停止追踪锚点。 * 可使用[HwArAnchor_release](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_anchor-0000001060493630#section187881818104820)释放锚点应用关联的内存。|

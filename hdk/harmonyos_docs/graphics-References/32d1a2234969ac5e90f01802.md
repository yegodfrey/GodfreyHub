|Enum Info|
|:--------|

|ACCESS_DEPTH_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT_READ_BIT = 0x00000200 深度模板附件读取位。|
|ACCESS_DEPTH_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT_WRITE_BIT = 0x00000400 深度模板附件写入位。|

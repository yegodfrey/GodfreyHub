### HwArAugmentedImage_getExtentX

|Function|
|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|HwArAugmentedImage_getExtentX(const [HwArSession](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_struct_session-0000001060385725) *session, const [HwArAugmentedImage](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_struct_augmentedimage-0000001060653626) *augmentedImage, float *outExtentX) 以[HwArAugmentedImage_getCenterPose](#section7121755695)获取图像的中心点为坐标原点，获取在X轴上估算出的物理图像的宽度（以米为单位）。AR Engine根据其对世界的理解不断的更新估算的物理图像宽度。当使用[HwArAugmentedImageDatabase_addImage](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/graphics-References/ndk_augmentedimagedatabase-0000001061131406#section1435415812424)指定了图像的物理尺寸，这个估算过程将更快收敛。 如果图像的Tracking状态为PAUSE或STOPPED，则返回的宽度信息是上一次跟踪时的宽度；如果图像从未被Tracking，即便指定了图像的物理尺寸，也返回0。|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:-------------|:------------------------|
|session|与AR Engine服务交互的Session对象。|
|augmentedImage|待估算宽度的图像。|
|outExtentX|估算的图像宽度。|

|Function|
|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

**Parameters**

|Name|Description|
|:-------------|:------------------------|
|session|与AR Engine服务交互的Session对象。|
|augmentedImage|待估算高度的图像。|
|outExtentZ|估算的图像高度。|

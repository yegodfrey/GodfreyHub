|int|[RemoveKeyThreads](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/graphics-References/removekeythreads-0000001055266380)(std::vector<int32_t> &tids) 移除关键线程。|

|int|[UnRegisterSystemEventCallback](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/graphics-References/unregistersys-0000001055346399)() 解注册系统事件回调函数。|

|int|[UnRegisterPerformanceTracer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/development/graphics-References/unregisterper-0000001055746348)() 取消注册性能追踪回调函数。|

# Getting Started

#### Quick Tutorial

Before developing your app, you can follow instructions in the [codelab](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/codelabsPortal/carddetails/HMSHEMKit) to experience how to quickly integrate this kit into an app.  

#### Development Environment

* JDK version: 1.7 or later

<!-- -->

* Android Studio version: 3.X or later
  * minSdkVersion: 19 or later
  * targetSdkVersion: 33 (recommended)
  * compileSdkVersion: 33 (recommended)
  * Gradle: 4.1 or later (recommended)

<!-- -->

* Test device:

A Huawei phone or tablet (running EMUI 12 or later)  

#### Development Process

Follow the steps in the following process when developing your app.  

|No.|Step|Description|
|:--|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|1|[Registering as a Developer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem-config-agc-0000001062384180)|Configure app information in AppGallery Connect.|
|2|[Integrating the HEM SDK](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem-integrating-sdk-0000001061424215)|Integrate the HEM SDK into your app.|
|3|[Configuring Network Permission](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem-configuring-network-permissions-0000001061974135)|To allow HTTP and HTTPS network requests on devices with targetSdkVersion 28 or later, configure network permission in the AndroidManifest.xml file.|
|4|[App Development](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem-apply-license-0000001058937093)|Develop your app as needed, [apply for an MDM license](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem-apply-license-0000001058937093), and then [activate or deactivate the license](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem-dpc-activation-deactivation-0000001058659007).|
|5|[(Optional) On-Cloud Development](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/hem_rest_preparation-0000001135700295)|The task of performing server development is necessary when you need to call APIs of the HEM Kit server (for example, you need to call the API to obtain the device SN) or some complex service logic needs to be implemented but your app cannot achieve it by itself.|
|6|[Pre-release Check](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/pre-release-check-0000001094941954)|Use the tool offered by Huawei to automatically check your app before release.|
|7|[App Release](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/doc/system-Guides/app-release-0000001194591538)|Complete your app information in AppGallery Connect, and submit your app for release.|
